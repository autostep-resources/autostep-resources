THE QUESTION

Did the workflow get better?

Count the work around production, review, correction, routing, and reporting. A higher volume of drafts is not the same as less work or a better business outcome.

STEP 03 / COMPARE OPTIONS AND DECIDE

Price the work.
Set the evidence bar.

Estimate direct workflow labor by role, then add relevant incremental tool, setup, and ongoing review costs. Count shared costs once.

Monthly labor cost = sum of (monthly executions × active minutes per execution ÷ 60 × loaded hourly rate by role)
Capacity released is not automatically cash saved. It becomes a financial saving only if it changes spend, overtime, contractor use, hiring, or the amount of work completed.
