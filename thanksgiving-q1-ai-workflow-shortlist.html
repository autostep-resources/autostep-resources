The AI Workflow Shortlist

A practical 30-minute session to compare three real workflows, surface the human work around AI, and choose one bounded test—or park the idea with a clear reason.

01 · 5 MINName three workflows
02 · 10 MINCapture effort and outcomes
03 · 10 MINCompare the simplest options
04 · 5 MINChoose a test or park it
01

Choose three real workflows

Use work that repeats and has an owner. Avoid starting with a tool or model.

Finance and fintech prompts: document follow-up, reconciliation, recurring reporting. These are starting points; rank them using your own workflow evidence.

03

Compare the intervention, not just the tool

Start with the least complex change that addresses the observed bottleneck.

01 · REMOVECan a step or duplicate handoff go away?
02 · SIMPLIFYCan the process or intake be clearer?
03 · STANDARDIZECan inputs and ownership be consistent?
04 · USE WHAT EXISTSCan current software handle the stable part?
05 · AUTOMATE RULESAre the steps repeatable with clear conditions?
06 · TEST AIDoes variable input or judgment make AI useful?
04

Bound one test—or park it

Optional capacity check: volume × (current minutes − observed minutes after change) ÷ 60 = hours changed in the period. Use comparable work and include review, rework, exceptions, and ongoing costs before making a financial conclusion.
