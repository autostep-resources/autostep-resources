WHAT YOU'LL DO

01 / Define done

Choose one recurring workflow, name its owner, and write what an accepted output must get right.

02 / Set a baseline

Record volume, hands-on time, waiting, corrections, handoffs, and exceptions over a clear period.

03 / Count the cost

Estimate direct labor by role, then add relevant incremental tool, setup, and ongoing review costs.

04 / Choose a test

Compare simpler fixes first, define a bounded test, and decide what evidence would mean go, revise, or stop.
